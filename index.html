Save to Google Photos Album

Save any image on the web to Google Photos — and choose the album it goes into — right from the right-click menu.

Add to Chrome Privacy Policy

Available on Chrome and other Chromium browsers.

What it does

  • Right-click any image and open Save to Google Photos Album.
  • Save to your library, an existing album, or a new album you create on the spot.
  • Unlike most tools, you pick the exact album — not just the main library.
  • Works with regular images, responsive images, and image links.
  • Images upload straight to your Google Photos — no third-party server, no tracking.

How it works

  1. Install the extension and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Right-click an image on any web page.
  3. Open Save to Google Photos Album and choose a destination.
  4. The image is saved to your Google Photos. Done.

Privacy

The extension can't browse your Google Photos or read your photos. It only uploads the images you choose and lists the albums it created. Nothing is sent to any third-party server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
