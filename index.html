Save to Google Photos Album

Right-click an image and save it to the Google Photos album of your choice.*

  • Due to Google Photos API limitations, you can only save to albums created with this extension.
  • Some image hosts may require one-time permission before saving.
  • Available for Chrome and Chromium-based browsers.
