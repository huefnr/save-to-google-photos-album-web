Save to Google Photos Album

Refund Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Scope and Payment Provider

Autonomous Affairs develops and operates Save to Google Photos Album. Basic plan is free. Plus plan is an optional paid subscription. Paddle acts as merchant of record and handles payments, invoices, and refund requests.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Open the support link in your Paddle receipt, or visit Paddle.net and choose Request refund.
  2. Use your billing email and provide your receipt or transaction reference and the reason for your request.
  3. Follow Paddle's instructions and check your email for the outcome.

For product problems or help locating a purchase, contact support. Do not send full card details, passwords, or Google access tokens.

When to Request a Refund

Ask Paddle within 14 days of the transaction date for a discretionary refund. Approval is not guaranteed. Statutory withdrawal and defect rights may have different deadlines and take precedence. Check the Paddle Refund Policy for the rules that apply to your purchase.

Eligibility and Consumer Rights

Refund requests are handled under the Paddle Refund Policy, the Paddle Buyer Terms, and applicable law. A request is not a guarantee of approval. Mandatory rights, including rights relating to faulty or misdescribed software, are not limited by this page. Where applicable law gives you stronger rights, those rights prevail.

Any applicable withdrawal period is separate from any free trial. See Pricing and checkout for the current trial offer, and the Paddle Refund Policy for withdrawal periods, conditions and exceptions that apply to your purchase. We do not treat the free trial as a waiver of these rights.

Cancellation Is Different from a Refund

Canceling prevents future renewal; it does not automatically refund a past payment. Use the management link in your Paddle receipt or Paddle Buyer Support. Cancel before a free trial ends to avoid its first annual charge. Cancel before renewal to avoid the next annual charge.

Access After Cancellation or Refund

Cancellation scheduled for the end of a trial or paid term normally leaves Plus plan available until that date. An approved full refund can end Plus plan access earlier. When Plus plan ends, the account returns to the Basic plan and you can choose up to 10 app-created albums to use in the extension. Images and albums are not deleted from Google Photos.

Refund Processing

Paddle handles an approved refund through the payment provider. Processing time depends on Paddle, the payment method, and the financial institution. Follow the status and instructions in Paddle's response; we do not promise a fixed arrival date.
