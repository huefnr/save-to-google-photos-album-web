Overview

Autonomous Affairs, a registered sole proprietorship in South Korea, operates Save to Google Photos Album and is responsible for the application data described here. Contact: support@save-to-album.com. Images are transferred directly from your browser to Google and never pass through our server. A subscription service provides Basic and Plus account features. Plus purchases are not yet open; payment processing described below applies when Plus launches.

What the extension accesses

When you sign in with Google, the extension requests only:

  • Permission to add to your Google Photos (upload the images you choose and create albums);
  • Permission to view the albums this extension created (to list them so you can pick one); and
  • Your email address (shown in the popup so you know which account is connected).

It handles only the specific images you choose to save.

Website access

  • The extension does not automatically read all websites by default. Temporary tab access, individual host permissions, optional all-website access, and site-specific right-click restoration are described below.
  • When you choose "Save to Google Photos Album" from the right-click menu, Chrome gives the extension temporary access to that one tab (the activeTab permission). It uses this only to find the image you right-clicked, show its dialog and result message, and read that image. The access ends when the tab navigates away or is closed.
  • To read the image, the extension first asks the page itself to load it, in the same way the page already displays it (including the site's own cookies for its own images). The extension does not read, store, or send the site's cookies or any other page content.
  • Browser access rules may prevent reading an image with the current permissions. The extension then asks for that image host (for example cdn.example.com) by default, and Chrome may ask you to confirm.
  • You may explicitly choose access to all HTTP/HTTPS websites in the permission window or extension Settings. This is a broad permission to read and change website data, not only images. The extension uses it for image saves you request and does not automatically inject scripts into all websites. You can turn broad access off in Settings; site-by-site prompts may return. Host authentication and download restrictions still apply.
  • If you enable persistent right-click restoration on a site, a guard may run on matching pages and frames after navigation to keep the native menu available. The site preference is stored locally. This feature does not bypass login or access to private posts.

Data stored by the extension

Stored locally in your browser: the Google sign-in tokens, the email address and Google account ID of the signed-in account (so the sign-in can be renewed without asking again), a cached list of the albums this extension created, and a short-lived cache of your Basic or Plus entitlement. Your appearance (theme) preference is stored with Chrome Storage Sync and may sync between Chrome browsers signed in to the same Google account.

Account and subscription service

When the extension checks your plan, it sends a short-lived Google OpenID Connect ID token over HTTPS to the subscription service hosted on Cloudflare. This identity token confirms which Google account is making the request but does not grant the service access to Google Photos. It is validated and discarded; it is not stored.

The service stores only:

  • Your stable Google account identifier (not your Google password);
  • Paddle customer and subscription identifiers, plan status, price identifier, renewal or cancellation effective date;
  • Up to 10 Google Photos album identifiers you choose to keep active on Basic, and identifiers for app-created albums you hide using Plus preferences; and
  • Webhook event identifiers used to prevent Paddle events from being processed twice.

The service does not store album names, images, browsing history, Google Photos access tokens, card details, billing addresses, or tax identifiers.

Data not collected

The extension cannot browse your Google Photos library or read your existing photos. It does not collect your browsing history, search queries, behavioral analytics, or advertising identifiers. The subscription service receives no website content or images.

Children

This is a general-audience service and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the developer learns that such information has been collected, it will be deleted. A parent or guardian may request deletion at support@save-to-album.com.

How Google user data is used

Google user data obtained through the extension (the images you choose to save, the albums it created, and your email address) is used solely to provide the feature you invoke: uploading the chosen image to your Google Photos, creating or selecting an album for it, and showing which account is connected. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or to develop, improve, or train any AI or machine-learning models.

Data sharing, transfer, and disclosure

The developer does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes. Service providers process limited data only to deliver the extension:

  • Images you save are uploaded directly from your browser to Google's Photos Library API (photoslibrary.googleapis.com). Google identity information is used to show the connected account and authenticate subscription requests. When you initiate a Plus checkout, your email may be passed to Paddle to prefill checkout. Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the subscription API and database and processes the identity and subscription fields described above.
  • Paddle acts as merchant of record for Plus. Paddle processes checkout, payment method, billing address, tax, invoices, refunds, and subscription management under the Paddle Privacy Notice. The developer does not receive full card details.
  • To save an image, the extension downloads it from the website that hosts it, then uploads it directly to Google. Neither Cloudflare nor Paddle receives that image.
  • There are no advertising networks or behavioral tracking technologies. Optional operational diagnostics are described below.

Data protection

The extension protects the sensitive data it handles as follows:

  • Authentication uses Google's OAuth 2.0 flow via the Chrome identity API. The extension never sees or stores your Google password.
  • The Google Photos OAuth access token is kept only in Chrome's extension-private storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never sent to the subscription service. The service receives a separate identity-only ID token that cannot access Google Photos.
  • The token is short-lived (it expires within about one hour and is re-obtained through Google when needed). No refresh token is stored.
  • All network traffic between the extension, Google, the subscription service, and Paddle uses HTTPS/TLS. Paddle webhook signatures are verified before subscription state is changed.
  • The extension requests only the minimum scopes needed: it can add images and create albums, and can list only the albums it created. It cannot read, browse, or download your existing photos or albums.
  • Image saves require your action. Background tasks can renew account state, refresh plan information, send opted-in diagnostics, or restore right-click on sites you enabled.

Optional reliability diagnostics

Disabled by default. In extension Settings, you may share operation results, allowlisted error codes, version and timing to help detect failures and release regressions. Reports exclude images, album names, email addresses, browsing URLs, page content, Google Photos access tokens and exception text. An existing short-lived identity-only token authenticates reporting; its identity is not stored with diagnostic aggregates. Reporting never opens a sign-in window.

Local queues are bounded. Reports expire for transmission after 24 hours and are cleared when the extension next runs; a closed browser cannot perform physical deletion. Disabling clears queued reports. Random batch receipts prevent duplicates and expire after seven days. Server aggregates expire after 30 days. Metrics cover reporting users only, not all users. Backend reliability and uptime are also monitored, and alerts without customer data are emailed to the developer. Cloudflare processes connection metadata such as IP addresses when handling requests; the application does not add these to diagnostic tables. Cloudflare operational log retention is separate.

Data retention and deletion

  • Access token, signed-in account (email and account ID), album list, and entitlement cache: Kept on your device. They are deleted when you click Sign out in the popup (which also revokes the access token with Google) or when you remove the extension. Tokens additionally expire on their own within about one hour.
  • Subscription service record: Retained while needed to provide Basic or Plus, resolve billing issues, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion after canceling Plus; data no longer required for those purposes will be deleted within 30 days.
  • Payment and transaction records: Retained by Paddle according to its legal and tax obligations and privacy notice.
  • Theme preference: Kept in Chrome Storage Sync until you remove the extension.
  • Access to image hosts you allowed: Kept by Chrome until you remove it on the extension's details page or remove the extension. The extension keeps no record of the sites you visited.
  • Images and albums saved to Google Photos: Stored by Google in your own Google Photos account, not by the developer. You can view, edit, or delete them at any time in Google Photos; deletion there is governed by Google's retention practices.
  • Revoking access: You can revoke the extension's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately; the extension will simply ask you to sign in again if you use it afterwards.

Website and support

GitHub and our infrastructure providers may process connection metadata needed to serve and protect it; see the GitHub Privacy Statement and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. If you send the draft from your email app, we receive your message, sender address and any attachments you add through our email providers at contact@autonomousaffairs.com. Do not include passwords, tokens or full card numbers. Support correspondence and attachments are kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations.

Email our support address to exercise these rights.
