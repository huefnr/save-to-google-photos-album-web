Can I Save to an Existing Google Photos Album?

No. The extension can only save to albums created with this extension, due to Google Photos API restrictions. Albums created directly in Google Photos or with another app cannot be used as save destinations.

To keep your existing collection together and continue saving with the extension, we recommend this one-time setup:

  1. Create a new album with this extension, using the same name as your existing album.
  2. In Google Photos, open the old album, select all its photos and videos, and add them to the new album.
  3. Check that everything is in the new album. You can then delete the old album to avoid having two albums with the same name.

From then on, choose the new album when saving with the extension. Using the same name does not connect or merge the two albums.

Delete only the old album, not its photos or videos. Adding items to another album does not require downloading or re-uploading them. Sharing links and comments from the old album do not transfer, so keep it if you still need them.

Can I Save Without Choosing an Album?

Yes. Choose Save to Library from the image's right-click menu to save directly to your Google Photos library without adding it to an album.

Why Does the Extension Ask for Website Access?

Some images are hosted on a different domain or cannot be downloaded with temporary tab access alone. The extension asks for access to the image host when needed. Site-by-site access is the default; access to all websites is optional. Permission does not bypass a site's login or download restrictions.

Why Can't I Right-Click an Image?

Some websites block the native right-click menu. In extension version 0.16.18 or later, open Settings, find Image Access, and turn on Automatically Unblock Right-Click. Once enabled, it applies automatically on future visits. If right-click is still blocked on an already-open page, reload it.

This option is off by default and requires permission to access all HTTP/HTTPS websites, shared with Allow All Websites. Allowing website access alone does not turn automatic restoration on. You can turn restoration off without removing image access; revoking all-website access also turns restoration off.

Restoration may replace a website's custom menu and cannot work on every page. It does not bypass sign-in or paid access. To allow just one site instead, open the extension on that page and choose Unblock Right-Click on This Site under Image Access when offered.

Can the Extension See My Existing Photos?

No. It cannot browse your existing Google Photos library. It uploads the images you choose and lists albums created with this extension. Images go directly from your browser to Google, not through our server. See our Privacy Policy for details about account data and optional diagnostics.

Do I Need a Subscription?

No. Basic plan is free and lets you save to up to 10 app-created albums. Plus plan is planned for users who need more album destinations, but is not yet available for purchase. See Plans for details.

How Do I Request a Refund?

  1. Open the support link in your Paddle receipt, or visit Paddle.net and choose Request refund.
  2. Use your billing email and provide your receipt or transaction reference and the reason for your request.
  3. Follow Paddle's instructions and check your email for the outcome.

Canceling a subscription does not automatically refund a past payment. See our Refund Policy for more details.

How Long Do I Have to Request a Refund?

Ask Paddle within 14 days of the transaction date for a discretionary refund. Approval is not guaranteed. Statutory withdrawal and defect rights may have different deadlines and take precedence. Check the Paddle Refund Policy for the rules that apply to your purchase.

Will I Lose My Saved Images If I Uninstall the Extension?

No. Images and albums already saved to Google Photos stay in your Google account. Removing the extension does not delete them.
